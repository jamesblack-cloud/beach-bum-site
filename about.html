Our Story

CRAFTED FOR THE GOOD LIFE.

There’s a stretch of sand where the afternoon stops moving. Sun on your shoulders, a slow bead of sweat at your temple, salt in the air, and the water four steps away — close enough that you could be in it whenever you feel like it, and no reason on earth to hurry.

That’s the place we had in mind, and every decision follows from it. We hand select premium whole flower and roll every one with care — no trim, no shake. Slow-burning, unhurried, built for long afternoons and the kind of evening that arrives without you noticing.

Proudly Canadian, and made for wherever you find your own patch of sand — a dock, a back deck, a lake in July. The beach is a feeling. You don’t need an ocean for it.
